# Milestone 2: a car on a 3D street, with sound

Acceptance (spec section 9): a 3-shot spec (chase, side drift, top-down) gives one video with sound; subject-visible and ground-present pass on all three shots.

## Result
- `projects/m2-street/project.json`: 3 shots (chase-01 2.5 s, drift-02 2.0 s, top-03 2.0 s), seed 5, 8 samples.
- Output `projects/m2-street/out/m2-street.mp4`: 156 frames, 6.50 s, 1920x1080 at 24 fps, with music, cues and one subtitle; loudness -13.7 LUFS, true peak -2.9 dBFS.
- All 15 checks pass (5 per shot). Contact sheet: `projects/m2-street/out/contact.png`.
- `tests/e2e/m2.test.ts` passes 4/4 (warm cache); 32 unit tests pass; typecheck clean.
- Cold cost on this machine: about an hour (3 Blender launches at ~100 s shader compile each, ~13 s per frame, ID passes, repeatable checks).

## What was hard
- **Blender on an Intel UHD.** EEVEE works headless but compiles shaders for ~100 s on the first frame of every launch. Vulkan and parallel compilation did not help, so shots are rendered one process per shot.
- **Making the set look drawn, not rendered.** Toon shading (diffuse into a constant colour ramp), Freestyle outlines, and keeping Freestyle from drawing lines through geometry below the ground (buildings follow the ground plane).
- **Effects that behave differently in the real scene than in a probe.** Per-object alpha through Object Info and Attribute nodes did not work in EEVEE here, and Track-To billboards did not render. Smoke now fades by scale with a constant-alpha material, and puffs are keyed to face the camera each frame.
- **Reproducibility.** The repeatable check failed three different ways: PNG metadata (M1), 2 pixels off by 1/255 when a lone frame was rendered (fixed by rendering frames 1..N in order, as the full render does), and then GPU contention and a driver crash (below).
- **Driver crash at exit.** The Intel OpenGL driver (`ig9icd64.dll`) sometimes crashes inside ExitProcess after Blender has written its result. The adapter keeps the result only when the exit code is 0xC0000005, the log says "Blender quit" and a fresh result file exists.
- **Two Blender runs at once share the GPU.** Run in parallel they went from ~13 s to ~95 s per frame, so the repeatable check runs them one after the other.

## Two weakest things
1. **It does not look like `car.mp4`.** This is a flat toon street with a low-poly car and no characters. The reference is painted. Painted locations (M1) and 3D sets (M2) are two separate looks, and nothing yet blends them. Characters are M5.
2. **Slow and fragile on this hardware.** An hour cold, a startup cost per launch that cannot be removed here, and a GPU driver that crashes on exit. The workaround is narrow and the repeatable check is slower than the shot it checks (it renders 30 frames twice). Smoke fading by scale is also a compromise: puffs shrink away rather than fade.
